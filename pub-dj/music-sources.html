Keep discovering

Search by song, artist, genre or mood. Use the genre filter, or ask the music assistant for suggestions from the room’s current collection. The helper uses local catalogue matching; it does not call an external AI service, make unavailable songs playable or approve music rights.

A weekly GitHub source check prepares candidate reports from eight supplier feeds and discovery pages. Only freely available recordings are considered. Automatic checks flag new candidates and missing information without calling an AI provider. Reports do not publish songs automatically: new tracks need licence, attribution and playback checks first.

Weekly source-check reports

Optional free P2P imports

Live hosts can import authorised audio using the built-in WebTorrent client. Hosts can choose an existing device VPN or a direct connection; this webpage does not provide or verify VPN protection. Transfers require compatible peers or HTTPS web seeds. Internet Archive torrent candidates are checked weekly but require artist and licence verification before use.

YouTube fallback

When a catalogue search has no match, a link opens matching results on the official YouTube site for personal browsing. YouTube results are not imported into the venue queue, extracted as audio or supplied as pub playback rights.
