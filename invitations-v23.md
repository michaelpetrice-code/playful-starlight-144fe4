The Local v23 — clearer invitations

Community: Account → Join Community. Copy/share community invite links from Account.
Private: Private Chats → Join Private Chat. Creators can copy the private code or a direct private join link.
Private join links open the Private Chats section and prefill the code; community join links open Account and prefill the community code.
Codes are held in URL fragments (not query strings) and removed from the address bar when the app starts. Share links privately.
Existing groups and messages are unchanged. No database migrations are required.
