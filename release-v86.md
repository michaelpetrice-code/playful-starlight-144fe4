# The Local v86 — Original Pub Arcade Expansion

Added two playable, original pub games inspired by the range of pub games on OnlyGames: **Five Dice (Yatzy-style)** with 13 scoring categories, holds, three rolls per round, and **Pub Pinball** with canvas physics, bumpers, flipper controls, score, and three balls. Existing games and marketplace preserved.

This is not a clone of OnlyGames and does not include its proprietary games, graphics, advertisements or backend. The new games are local single-player games; online multiplayer, leaderboards and further OnlyGames-style game categories are not implemented. Pinball is an original lightweight arcade game, not a commercial physics simulation.

Test on a staging Netlify deployment before replacing the live site.
