The Local v63 — redesigned Games Room. Twelve graphical game tiles. Existing functional games: 8 Ball Pool, Darts, Battleships (solo), Noughts & Crosses (online with Supabase setup), Connect Four (online with Supabase setup), Word Search, Crossword, Block Puzzle, Pub Quiz. Added simple on-device variants: Snooker Practice, High Card (Solitaire tile), Ludo Race. These three are NOT full snooker, Klondike solitaire or standard Ludo. Online multiplayer remains limited to Noughts & Crosses and Connect Four. Visual design uses CSS/SVG assets rather than the photorealistic mockup; not an exact reproduction.
