A few useful answers

Do members need to pay?

No. Member accounts are free. Venue subscriptions are separate.

Does this replace our ticket provider?

You can publish your events and link to your existing ticket provider.

Does room hire include live availability?

The venue starter shows your room information and receives enquiries. Live room availability and automatic booking confirmation are not included.

Can you guarantee more customers?

No. The Local helps organise your venue information and enquiries. Results depend on your venue and how you use it.
