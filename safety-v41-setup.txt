THE LOCAL V41 — COMMITTEE SAFETY HARDENING
1. Run SAFETY-REPORTING-SETUP.sql from v40 if not previously installed.
2. Run SAFETY-V41-HARDENING.sql in The Local Supabase SQL Editor.
3. Add vetted committee reviewers to public.local_safety_committee by verified auth.users UID.
4. Deploy the entire lads-local-pwa folder to Netlify.
5. Test with two separate accounts: a member submits a report with a small image, an approved reviewer reads it and changes its status. Confirm a regular member cannot open the Committee Safety Inbox or read evidence.
6. Review the privacy notice, who can review reports, incident response process, retention period and appeals before inviting members.

IMPLEMENTED: private evidence uploads (5 files max, 10 MB each); committee-only signed viewing links; restricted committee review RPC; immutable original report content through client permissions; review audit entries; confidential form and status tracking.
NOT IMPLEMENTED: automatic emails, automatic escalation, automatic purging, moderation of live chat, evidence virus scanning. Urgent flags require a human to check the inbox. Do not claim the system is monitored 24/7.
For production, configure appropriate email notifications with a secure server-side provider, never a secret key in browser code. Decide retention and an independent route for complaints about committee members.
