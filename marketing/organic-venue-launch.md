## Three-minute demo script

1. Open the free venue demo. Enter the prospect’s venue name with their permission, select its type and change the colour theme. Show the preview.
2. Explain the core offer: their branding, public venue information, events with external ticket-provider links and an enquiry inbox.
3. Ask which of those solves their most immediate problem. Focus the discussion on that one feature.
4. Show the pricing and trial terms clearly. Distinguish the no-account demo from the payment-method-required subscription trial.
5. Invite them to start setup or ask questions. Do not promise automatic increases in attendance, sales or bookings.

## Quick answers

**Is it free for members?** Yes. Member accounts are free; the venue subscription is separate.

**Can I see it before signing up?** Yes. The public venue demo needs no account.

**Does it replace our ticket provider?** The advertised venue starter lets you publish events and link to your existing ticket provider.

**Is room hire a live booking calendar?** The advertised starter shows room information and receives enquiries. Do not describe it as live availability or automatic booking confirmation.

**Is the trial free without a card?** No payment is taken today, but a payment method is required. It renews automatically unless cancelled before the trial ends.

**Can you guarantee more customers?** No. The product helps organise venue information and enquiries; results depend on the venue and how it is used.
