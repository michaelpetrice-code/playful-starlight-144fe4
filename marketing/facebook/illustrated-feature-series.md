# The group chat can wait

Lead with the AI-created A seat at The Local artwork. Follow with the feature images and captions below. For Facebook Page use @followers when selectable. For Facebook Groups use @everyone where permitted. Adapt the stills for Instagram, WhatsApp Status, Threads, Bluesky and X. For Reels, TikTok and Shorts, group three cards into a 15-second vertical edit. Use the owner-facing Quiz Pro and Builder captions for LinkedIn and venue newsletters. Keep promotions organic. Premium products have their own terms.
