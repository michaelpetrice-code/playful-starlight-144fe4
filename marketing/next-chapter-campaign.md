# The Local: Your Next Chapter

An AI-created organic campaign built in GitHub. The creative hook is a venue's own identity: instead of reading a generic sales pitch, visitors make a personalised next-chapter concept they can take away.

## Experience

Campaign route: `/venue-future.html`. Entry from `/for-your-venue.html`.

1. Enter a public venue name, choose venue type, goal and colour.
2. Generate a concept card, suggested public launch message and seven-day practical plan.
3. Download an SVG concept card or text kit, copy the message, open the free venue demo, or draft a walkthrough request.

The generator uses curated templates, not a runtime AI service. It sends no form data, uses no API keys and stores no personalisation. It creates no accounts, trials or live apps. Message copy explicitly identifies a concept. The SVG download escapes input; rendered text uses textContent. There is no automatic social publishing.

## Seven organic campaign angles

1. Origin story: ask venue owners what their venue means to its regulars; link to the studio.
2. Before the doors open: show the studio using an explicitly fictional venue name.
3. Name in lights: invite owners to make their concept card. Do not imply it is a published app.
4. One real need: focus on events, room enquiries or accurate venue information.
5. Behind the build: show the source and explain that the studio needs no paid AI APIs.
6. Community question: ask what one detail visitors wish venues made easier to find.
7. Demo invitation: offer a personal walkthrough; explain pricing before a trial.

These are editorial angles, not scheduled or published posts. Publish at a pace appropriate to the audience, using permitted channels. No unsolicited bulk messages, fake customer results, fabricated testimonials, unapproved customer names or paid boosting.

## Success measurement

First measure replies and qualified walkthrough requests using native channel insights and the existing enquiry channel. Campaign links carry organic UTM parameters into the demo. Do not claim concept completion, revenue attribution or conversion tracking is implemented; this studio intentionally has no new tracking. No sales or attendance results are guaranteed.

## Validation

Node tests cover all 54 combinations, invalid configuration fallbacks, name limits, SVG escaping and long-name layout. Existing static checks cover public assets and guest routes. Browser checks should cover narrow/mobile view, form generation, copy, downloads and the demo link before production promotion.
