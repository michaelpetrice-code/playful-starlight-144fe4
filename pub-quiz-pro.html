How quiz night works

Set your questions

Build up to 20 rounds with your own topics and up to 60 multiple-choice questions. Set the correct answers before you open the lobby.

Bring the teams in

Share a quiz code or join link. Each captain registers a team and submits answers from one phone.

Reveal the leaderboard

Control when each question opens and closes. The Local scores the teams as you reveal the answers.

Hosting for registered venues

Hosting is included with an active paid venue subscription when your approved venue is linked to your Venue App Builder account. Players can join free with a Local account.
