# The Local — free discovery listing kit

Official website: https://thelocal.me.uk/

Name: The Local
Tagline: Your local community, all in one place

Short description: Discover venues, join virtual pub tables, find events, and connect with your local community.

Full description:
The Local brings pubs, social clubs, venues and their communities together in one place. Explore participating venues, discover events, connect with people at virtual pub tables and browse community marketplace listings. Join for free and use The Local on your phone or desktop. Venue owners can explore tools for managing and growing their communities.

Suggested categories: Social networking; Communities; Events; Local discovery.
Pricing: Free membership; optional paid venue subscriptions. Verify live offer before publishing.

## Free launch destinations
- Google Search Console: https://search.google.com/search-console
- Bing Webmaster Tools: https://www.bing.com/webmasters
- Product Hunt: https://www.producthunt.com/

## Before publishing
- Confirm guest journey, sign-up, login, install prompt and links work on mobile.
- Verify /sitemap.xml and /robots.txt on the deployed preview.
- Check the public page title, description, Open Graph preview, canonical URL and share image.
- Provide real screenshots of the working product, not mockups presented as screenshots.
- Submit sitemap to verified Google and Bing properties; these steps require site-owner account access.
- Product Hunt submission requires a personal account, logo and at least one gallery image.
- Never publish credentials, private member data or administrator-only URLs in listings.

## Installation instructions
Android (Chrome): Open https://thelocal.me.uk/ and choose Install app or Add to Home screen from the browser menu, when offered.
iPhone (Safari): Open https://thelocal.me.uk/, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

## Scope
This branch prepares discovery assets only. It does not submit listings or change the live website.
