What you need

A host laptop, tablet or phone connected to the venue speakers, an internet connection for the shared queue, and audio files the venue is authorised to use. Import MP3, WAV, OGG or M4A files, up to 500 songs and 50 MB per file. Playback support depends on the device and audio format.

Use one playback tab. After reopening it, reselect the same music files and claim playback on that device. Rooms close after 12 hours. The separate screen view stays silent.

Music supply, recording rights and venue music permissions are separate from the software price. Personal Spotify subscriptions do not authorise pub playback. The three included original sample tracks let you try the controls.
